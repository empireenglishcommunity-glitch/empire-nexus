> **Owner approval gate.** This document states exactly how the C2 level maps
> to the CEFR, so the alignment is auditable before C2 goes live. C2 content
> loads into the engine but nothing changes for students until they complete
> C1 and pass the C1 exit exam — and until you sign off on C2. **C2 is the

## Truth-in-labelling

C2 content is **CEFR-aligned** (built to the official CEFR C2 descriptors). On
completion, Empire English issues its own certificate: *"Empire English
certifies that [name] has demonstrated proficiency at CEFR Level C2."* We do
**not** claim "CEFR certified" — CEFR has no certifying body.

## What CEFR says C2 is (the target)

> *Can understand with ease virtually everything heard or read. Can summarise
> information from different spoken and written sources, reconstructing
> arguments and accounts in a coherent presentation. Can express him/herself
> spontaneously, very fluently and precisely, differentiating finer shades of
> meaning even in the most complex situations.*
> — Council of Europe, CEFR global scale, C2. C2 is the top **proficient
> user** band — mastery: effortless, precise, stylistically controlled use of
> the language, differentiating the finest shades of meaning. (C2 is *not*
> "native-speaker" level; it is full operational mastery for real purposes.)

## How this C2 course delivers that

### 1. Can-Do objectives (learning outcomes)
`can_do.json` → `C2` holds 20 curated descriptors across all four CEFR modes:
- **Reception** (5): understand virtually any spoken language at fast native
  speed; read virtually any written text incl. abstract/complex/colloquial;
  appreciate fine points, humour, irony and implicit cultural meaning;
  distinguish finer shades of stance conveyed only stylistically; reconstruct
  precise detail from specialised or fragmented sources.
- **Production** (6): smoothly flowing, well-structured speech and text that
  helps the recipient notice/remember key points; well-supported expositions of
  complex subjects; precise expression differentiating finer shades of meaning;
  full control of style/register and stylistic devices; and reconstruction of
  multiple sources into a concise, precise whole.
- **Interaction** (5): effortless participation with idiom/colloquialism;
  spontaneous, very fluent, precise expression; skilful interweaving with
  natural turn-taking and allusion; seamless backtracking/repair; precise
  conveyance of nuance, emphasis, irony and emotion.
- **Mediation** (4): summarise and reconstruct across sources; reformulate
  complex material for different audiences; mediate sensitive exchanges
  diplomatically; clarify complex/implicit points, differentiating fine meaning.

Every week references the specific descriptors it targets; across the 20 weeks
**all 20 C2 descriptors** are covered.

This builds on C1 (inversion, cleft/fronting, participle clauses, nominalisation,
hedging, register, complex noun phrases) and takes each to the **mastery**
ceiling: the subjunctive, literary/stylistic inversion, grammatical metaphor,
full cleft mastery, irony/understatement, register mixing, rhetoric and
seamless repair. C2 is the top of the ladder — there is no level above it.

### 3. Vocabulary band (C2)
~620 active words across the 20 weeks, all upper-range / academic / literary C2
items selected against public CEFR-mapped references (Oxford 5000 C2 tags,
English Vocabulary Profile C2). Stored as **our own list** (word, pronunciation,
Arabic gloss, POS, example). Themes span the most demanding C2 domains: formal
subjunctive and literary conditionals, marked syntax, grammatical metaphor,
sophisticated concession, meta-discourse, epistemic stance, near-synonym
precision, connotation, idiom/proverb, allusion/wordplay, irony/understatement,
register control, rhetoric, rebuttal, concision, spontaneous repair, and a
mastery capstone.

CEFR guidance puts C2 vocabulary well above 8,000 words *receptive*; a ~620-word
curated **active** production layer for a 20-week mastery course, stacked on
A1–C1 (~2,400), is a sound C2 core (assessments test production).

### 4. Skills coverage (all five, every week)
Each week file provides: **vocabulary**, **listening** (dictation items),
**pronunciation/phonology** (`phoneme_focus` — expressive range, ironic and
rhetorical intonation, register-marked delivery, seamless repair), **speaking**
(7 daily missions, 45–120s, demanding rhetoric, nuance, register control and
sustained spontaneous discourse), and **writing** (7 prompts, incl. rhetorical,
stylistic-editing and register-shifting tasks). This matches CEFR C2's
mastery-level expectations across all four modes.

### 5. Progression logic
The 20 weeks build a coherent C2 mastery arc: **marked & literary syntax**
(subjunctive → literary conditionals → stylistic inversion → fronting → cleft
mastery → grammatical metaphor) → **precision & stance** (concession,
meta-discourse, epistemic stance, near-synonym, connotation) → **idiom, figure &
tone** (idioms/proverbs, allusion/wordplay, irony/understatement, register
mixing) → **rhetoric & argument** (persuasion, rebuttal) → **craft & fluency**
(concision/editing, spontaneous repair) → **mastery capstone**. Week 20 is an
explicit review + a 120-second mastery capstone that previews the C2 exit exam.

## Assessment alignment (handled by the Taqdeem engine)
- **Weekly** tests that week's can-do objectives + vocab + grammar.
- **Monthly** tests retention across the C2 weeks.
- **C2 exit exam** (Part A skills + Part B: an extended, stylistically
  controlled spoken and written response on a complex subject, differentiating
  finer shades of meaning) tests the C2 can-do set as a whole; passing certifies
  the student at **CEFR C2** — the top of the Empire English ladder.
