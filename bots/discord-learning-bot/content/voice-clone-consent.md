## What this is about

Empire English Community (EEC), operated under MACAL EMPIRE, produces an AI-powered English-learning podcast called **Empire Chronicles**. The podcast uses AI text-to-speech technology to generate spoken audio from written scripts. To make the podcast sound natural and personal, we use **voice cloning** — a technology that creates a digital replica of a person's voice from a short recording.

We are asking for your **explicit, informed consent** to clone your voice for use in this podcast.

---

## What we will do with your voice

1. **Create a digital voice clone** from one or more audio recordings you have provided (e.g., recordings in the EEC Discord showcase channel).
2. **Use your cloned voice** to generate spoken audio for podcast episodes published within the EEC Discord server and any official EEC distribution channels.
3. Your cloned voice will be used to voice **fictional characters** in the podcast — it does not represent your personal views or statements.

## What we will NOT do

1. We will **never** sell, license, or share your voice clone or recordings with any third party.
2. We will **never** use your cloned voice for any purpose outside of EEC educational content without obtaining separate written consent.
3. We will **never** use your voice to create content that is misleading, defamatory, or harmful to your reputation.
4. We will **never** represent your cloned voice as your actual live speech or statements.

## Your rights

1. **You can withdraw this consent at any time** by notifying the EEC team (via Discord message to the admin team or by email). Upon withdrawal:
   - Your voice clone will be **deleted** from all systems within 7 days.
   - No new content will be produced using your cloned voice after withdrawal.
   - Previously published episodes that already contain your cloned voice may remain published (since they are pre-recorded content), unless you specifically request their removal.
2. **You own your voice.** This consent grants EEC a limited, revocable license to use a clone of your voice for the stated purpose only. It does not transfer ownership of your voice or recordings.
3. You may **request a copy** of any recording of yours that EEC holds at any time.

## Duration

This consent is valid from the date signed until you withdraw it. There is no expiration date — it remains in effect until you say otherwise.
